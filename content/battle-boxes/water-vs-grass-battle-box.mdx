## The purest way to learn

This is our **Family & Friends** Battle Box — the pure-bulk tier. Two complete decks, balanced against each other, built entirely from **commons, uncommons, and regular rares: no `ex`, no Mega, no ACE SPEC, and no holo or chase cards.** Nothing here costs much or is hard to find, so it's the perfect, no-pressure way for two people to learn Pokémon TCG together.

Each deck teaches a different half of how the game is really won — and it's not always by hitting hardest:

- **Water (tempo & control)** teaches you to *slow your opponent down* — evolving Buizel into Floatzel and using Whirlpool to strip Energy off the board so they can never quite get going.
- **Grass (status & stall)** teaches you to *lock your opponent out* — growing the Wurmple lines and using Paralysis and Poison to keep them from acting while you chip away.

Play them against each other and you'll learn evolving (single- and multi-stage), branching evolution, energy management, and two ways to win that aren't just "deal the most damage." Then trade decks and see the other side.

## Play your first game together

Set both players up the same way — this shared setup works for either deck.

1. **Shuffle and draw 7 cards** each. Make sure you have at least one Basic Pokémon; if not, show your hand, reshuffle, and draw 7 again (your opponent draws an extra card).
2. **Put one Basic Pokémon in the Active spot** (Buizel or Wurmple), and up to five more on your Bench.
3. **Set 6 Prize cards** aside face down.
4. **Flip a coin to decide who goes first.**
5. **Take turns:** draw, attach one Energy, play Trainers/evolutions, then attack (or apply status). First player to take all 6 Prizes wins.
6. **Then trade decks and run it back** — tempo vs. status feels completely different from the other seat.

Because both decks are all-commons and non-`ex`, games are close and forgiving — exactly what you want when you're learning.
