## What this box teaches

The best way to learn Pokémon TCG is to play it — and the best way to play it is against someone else who's learning too. That's what the Battle Box is: **two complete decks, balanced against each other, each one teaching a different half of the game.**

- **Delphox (Fire)** teaches the "build up and unleash" side — evolving Pokémon, using an Ability every turn, and scaling your damage as the game goes on.
- **Fighting** teaches the "hit fast" side — attacking early, managing Energy under pressure, and racing for Prize cards.

Play them against each other a few times and you'll have hands-on experience with almost every core rule in the game: evolving, attaching Energy, using Abilities, choosing attacks, and taking Prizes. Then swap decks and see the game from the other side.

## Play your first game together

Set both players up the same way — this shared setup works for either deck.

1. **Shuffle and draw 7 cards** each. Make sure you have at least one Basic Pokémon in hand; if you don't, show your hand, reshuffle, and draw 7 again (your opponent draws an extra card when this happens).
2. **Put one Basic Pokémon in the Active spot** (face down until both players are ready), and up to five more on your Bench.
3. **Set your Prizes.** Put the top 6 cards of your deck aside face down — these are the Prize cards you'll take as you knock out the other player's Pokémon.
4. **Flip a coin to decide who goes first.** The Fighting deck loves going first (more pressure, sooner); Delphox is happy going either way.
5. **Take turns:** draw a card, attach one Energy, play any Trainers or evolutions you like, then attack. First player to take all 6 Prizes wins.
6. **Then trade decks and run it back.** Seeing both game plans is the fastest way to actually *get* how the game flows.

Every card in both decks is a real, tournament-legal card — so once you've learned the ropes here, you're ready to step up to the competitive builds in the deck index.
