## The Gimmick

The whole deck is a flip engine — here's how it plays out:

1. **Lead with Fury Swipes.** **Team Rocket's Meowth** flips 3 coins and does damage per heads — cheap, early, and swingy. It sets the tone.
2. **Stack coin-flip attackers.** The **Shinx → Luxio → Luxray** line and **Tyrunt → Tyrantrum** bring bigger flip-for-damage or flip-for-effect attacks. Build a bench of them so you always have a flip to make.
3. **Add utility flips.** **Heliolisk** and **Pikipek** round out the deck with more flip-based effects, so nearly every attack is a mini coin game.
4. **Gust and pray.** Use **Boss's Orders** to pull up a target, then flip for the knockout. When it hits, it feels amazing. When it whiffs… that's the bit.

## Why It's Fun

There's no salt like coin-flip salt — and no joy like ripping three heads to knock out something you had no business beating. This deck is pure variance: it turns every turn into a moment, and it's the perfect "let's just have fun" pick for a casual night. It's not for tournaments; it's for the table erupting when the coin lands your way.
