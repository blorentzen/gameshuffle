**Format:** Standard 2026 (Regulation Marks H / I / J)
**Archetype cost:** roughly $20–$30 in singles
**Difficulty:** Beginner — a friendly first deck built around one clear plan

Delphox is the "build up and unleash" deck. You spend the early turns getting set up — evolving your Fennekin, drawing extra cards with an Ability, and loading Fire Energy onto the board — and then Delphox hits harder and harder the longer the game goes. It's a great first deck because every turn has an obvious next step, and there's no complicated attack math to memorize.

This is one half of the **Fire vs. Fighting Battle Box** — two decks built to be played against each other so two new players can learn the game together. It also stands completely on its own.

> **New to the game?** This page is written for a first-time player. If a term is unfamiliar, just keep reading — every card here does one simple job, and the walkthrough below shows you the first few turns step by step.

## What This Deck Teaches You

Instead of "matchups," here's what you'll actually learn by playing Delphox — the fundamentals every Pokémon deck relies on:

- **Evolving.** You start with Fennekin (a Basic), then play Braixen on top of it, then Delphox on top of that. Watching a tiny Basic grow into a powerful Stage 2 is the core loop of the whole game.
- **Using an Ability.** Delphox's Ability, **Flaring Magic**, lets you discard a Fire Energy to draw until you have 7 cards. Abilities are "free" effects that don't cost your attack for the turn — learning to use one every turn is a huge step up.
- **Scaling damage.** Delphox's attack, **Energized Storm**, does 30 damage for each Energy in play. The more you build up, the harder you hit — so you learn *why* setup matters instead of just attacking on turn one.

## Your First Few Turns

A forgiving, plain-language game plan. You don't have to do this perfectly — just follow the steps.

1. **Get Fennekin down.** Put at least one Fennekin on your Bench (and one Active if you can). Use **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** or **Poké Ball** if you need to find them.
2. **Attach a Fire Energy every turn.** You get one Energy attachment per turn — never skip it. Getting Energy onto the board early is what powers Delphox later.
3. **Evolve when you can.** Fennekin → Braixen → Delphox. There's no rush and no penalty for taking your time.
4. **Once Delphox is out, use Flaring Magic first.** Discard one Fire Energy, draw up to 7 cards. Do this *before* you attack — it refills your hand for free.
5. **Then attack with Energized Storm.** Count the Energy in play, multiply by 30, and enjoy watching the number climb as the game goes on.
6. **Heal if you're getting low.** A **Potion** at the right moment can buy you the extra turn you need to knock out their Pokémon first.

## How to Play It

Delphox rewards patience. If your opening hand is a little slow, that's okay — this deck is built to catch up. Lean on **Naveen**, **Judge**, **Poké Pad**, and Delphox's own Ability to keep drawing cards, and remember that every Fire Energy you attach makes your attack bigger. When you want to close out the game, use **Boss's Orders** or **Pokémon Catcher** to drag up the opponent's weakest Pokémon and take a Prize card.
