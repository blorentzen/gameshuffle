**Format:** Standard 2026 (Regulation Marks H / I / J)
**Archetype cost:** roughly $12–$18 in singles
**Difficulty:** Beginner — a multi-stage evolution + status teaching deck

This is the "status & stall" half of the **Water vs. Grass Battle Box** — an all-commons kitchen-table deck that teaches you to lock your opponent down with status conditions. You grow Wurmple into Beautifly (or Dustox), then use status effects to keep the other player from acting while you chip away. It's a ~40-card intro deck built to learn on.

> **Family & Friends rule:** this deck contains **no `ex`, no Mega, no ACE SPEC, and no holo/chase cards** — commons, uncommons, and regular rares only. Affordable, sharable, and easy to learn.

## What This Deck Teaches You

- **Multi-stage evolution.** Wurmple → Silcoon → Beautifly (or Wurmple → Cascoon → Dustox) — a full three-stage line, one step up from a simple evolution.
- **Branching evolution.** Wurmple can become *two different* Pokémon — a great lesson that your choices mid-game matter.
- **Status conditions.** Paralysis and Poison (via Stun Spore / Dustox) teach you to win without just dealing damage — lock the opponent down and let the board do the work.

## Your First Few Turns

1. **Get Wurmple down** with **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** or **Ultra Ball**.
2. **Attach a Grass Energy every turn.**
3. **Decide your branch** — evolve toward **Beautifly** (offense + Paralysis) or **Dustox** (Poison + disruption).
4. **Apply status.** Paralyze or poison their Active Pokémon so they can't fight back cleanly.
5. **Chip and stall** until you've taken all your Prizes.

## How to Play It

You're the disruptive one. Against the Water deck in the box (which wants to control tempo), your job is to lock its attacker down with status so it can never set up its energy-denial rhythm. Patience wins.
