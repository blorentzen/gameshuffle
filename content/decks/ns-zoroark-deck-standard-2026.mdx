This is a nearly all-N build: N's Zoroark ex leads, backed by N's Reshiram, N's Zekrom, and the N's Darmanitan line, with the whole Trainer engine flying N's colors. It's a genuine attacker with a clean, flavorful game plan.

## Why This Deck Is All N

The draw here is the theme, and it holds up: nearly every card is an _N's_ card. **N's Zoroark ex** is your engine and main attacker; **N's Reshiram** and **N's Zekrom** are the legendary heavy hitters N is famous for; the **N's Darmanitan** line adds a Fire threat; and even the support — **N's PP Up**, **N's Castle** — carries his name. The only neutral cards are the format-standard draw/search staples every deck runs (Lillie's Determination, Boss's Orders, Ultra Ball, Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Night Stretcher).

## How to Play It

Get **N's Zorua** down early and evolve into **N's Zoroark ex** — that's your consistency and damage core. Use **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** and **Ultra Ball** to find your Basics, **Lillie's Determination** to keep drawing, and **Boss's Orders** to pull up the Pokémon you most want to knock out. Bring **N's Reshiram** or **N's Zekrom** online as your closers when you need a bigger swing.

> **Prize note — you're running an `ex`.** N's Zoroark ex gives up **two Prize cards** when knocked out. It's your engine, so protect it — don't leave it Active in front of a knockout you can't answer.
