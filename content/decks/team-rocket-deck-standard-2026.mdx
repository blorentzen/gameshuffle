## Why This Deck Is All Team Rocket

This is the draw. The list is **~95% Team Rocket cards** — the only non-Rocket slots are a handful of neutral search-and-recovery staples (Ultra Ball, Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Boss's Orders, Night Stretcher) that every deck plays. Everything that defines the deck is on-brand:

- **Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex** — a 280 HP Psychic attacker and the face of the deck.
- **Team Rocket's Persian ex** (via **Meowth**) — the crew's slick secondary threat.
- **Giovanni, Ariana, Proton, Petrel** — the actual Team Rocket admins, running your whole Trainer engine.
- **Team Rocket's Factory & Watchtower** — Rocket-flavored Stadiums.
- **Team Rocket's Energy** — even your Energy is on theme.

If you want to go *fully* purist you can, but the two `ex` Pokémon (Mewtwo ex, Persian ex) are what make the deck competitive. That's the one honest tradeoff to know about — see the prize note below.

## How to Play It

The engine is straightforward: **Proton** sets you up on turn one, **Ariana** and **Transceiver** keep the Rocket Supporters flowing, and **Team Rocket's Spidops** provides the Ability support that lets the deck run smoothly. Attack with **Mewtwo ex**, use **Giovanni** or **Boss's Orders** to drag up whatever you most want to knock out, and lean on **Persian ex** as your second threat when Mewtwo needs a break.

> **Prize note — you're running `ex` Pokémon.** Both Mewtwo ex and Persian ex give up **two Prize cards** when they're knocked out (instead of one). That's the price of their power — play around it by not over-committing `ex` attackers you don't need on the board yet.

## What to Buy (and What NOT to Buy)

You do **not** need the expensive alternate arts to play this deck. Buy the cheap, fully-legal playable prints:

- **Team Rocket's Mewtwo ex — DRI 81** (the Double Rare, roughly **$1.56**), *not* the Special Illustration Rare **DRI 231** (~$559). Same card in play, a fraction of the price.
- The same logic applies to **Persian ex** and the rest — grab the base Double Rare / standard prints, not the alt-art chase versions.
