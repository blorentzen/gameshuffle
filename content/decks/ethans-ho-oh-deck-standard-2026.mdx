## Why This Deck Is All Ethan

The theme carries the deck: almost every card is an _Ethan's_ card. **Ethan's Ho-Oh ex** is the payoff attacker, the **Ethan's Cyndaquil → Quilava → Typhlosion** line is the Fire engine that fuels it, and the **Ethan's Slugma → Magcargo** line rounds out the support — plus **Ethan's Adventure** for on-theme Trainer support. The only neutral cards are the format-standard draw and search staples every deck runs.

## How to Play It

This is a build-and-power-up Fire deck. Get the **Cyndaquil** line going early, evolve into **Typhlosion** to fuel your board with Fire Energy, and bring **Ho-Oh ex** online as your heavy hitter. Use **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** and **Ultra Ball** to find your Basics, **Lillie's Determination** to keep the cards flowing, and **Boss's Orders** to pick off the Pokémon you want gone. The high Fire count keeps your attachments and acceleration consistent.

> **Prize note — you're running an `ex`.** Ethan's Ho-Oh ex gives up **two Prize cards** when knocked out. It's your closer, so set it up behind a wall of evolving Pokémon rather than exposing it early.

Buy the cheap playable prints, not the alternate arts — the base Double Rare of Ethan's Ho-Oh ex plays identically to its expensive Special Illustration Rare.
