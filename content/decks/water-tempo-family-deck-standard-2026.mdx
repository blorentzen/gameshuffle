**Format:** Standard 2026 (Regulation Marks H / I / J)
**Archetype cost:** roughly $12–$18 in singles
**Difficulty:** Beginner — a clean, single-evolution teaching deck

This is the "tempo & control" half of the **Water vs. Grass Battle Box** — a simple, all-commons kitchen-table deck that teaches you to control the pace of the game. You evolve Buizel into Floatzel, then use Whirlpool to strip your opponent's Energy away so they can never quite get going. It's a ~40-card intro deck built to learn on, not a tournament list.

> **Family & Friends rule:** this deck contains **no `ex`, no Mega, no ACE SPEC, and no holo/chase cards** — commons, uncommons, and regular rares only. It's built to be affordable, sharable, and easy to learn.

## What This Deck Teaches You

- **Evolving.** Buizel → Floatzel — one clean step. A perfect first evolution to learn on.
- **Energy attachment.** One Water Energy per turn; the deck runs plenty so you always have one to play.
- **Tempo / energy denial.** Floatzel's **Whirlpool** (30 damage + discard an Energy) teaches the most important control idea in the game: slowing your opponent down is as good as dealing damage.

## Your First Few Turns

1. **Get a Buizel down.** Use **Buddy-Buddy Poffin** or **Ultra Ball** if you need to find one.
2. **Attach a Water Energy every turn.** Never skip it.
3. **Evolve into Floatzel** as soon as you can.
4. **Use Whirlpool to strip their Energy.** Every Energy you discard is a turn they can't attack — that's you controlling the game.
5. **When you're ahead, swing bigger** with Floatzel's Aqua Slash to close it out.

## How to Play It

Play patiently. Your job isn't to race — it's to keep your opponent one step behind by knocking their Energy off the board. Against the Grass deck in the box (which wants to lock you with status), controlling tempo is exactly how you stay in front.
